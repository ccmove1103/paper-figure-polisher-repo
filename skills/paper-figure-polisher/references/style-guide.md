# Style Guide

Use this guide when normalizing chart and figure formatting. For full manuscript layout, body typography, equation, and unit rules, also read `references/manuscript-style.md`.

## Source Status

This v1 profile is designed to be compatible with the user's requested SCI-style direction and is anchored to documented publication guidance where available. If a cited article gives more specific rules, prefer that article. If the article cannot be retrieved directly, treat this guide as an informed baseline and note the assumption.

## Typography

Apply these defaults unless the journal template overrides them:

- Use Times New Roman as the default English font for manuscript-facing output.
- Use `宋体` for Chinese body text and `黑体` for Chinese heading emphasis when the manuscript includes Chinese.
- Keep text consistent across axis labels, tick labels, legends, panel labels, and annotations.
- Prefer final printed sizes that remain readable after scaling.
- Avoid tiny labels, mixed fonts, or decorative type.

## Lines and Shapes

- Use consistent axis and curve line weights across related figures.
- Make panel labels visually clear and consistent.
- Avoid excessive border thickness.
- Remove heavy boxes, drop shadows, 3D effects, and glossy fills.

## Color

- Use a restrained palette.
- Prefer high-contrast, colorblind-safer combinations.
- Keep category colors consistent across all figures in the manuscript.
- Do not encode key differences with color alone when labels or patterns are needed.

## Chart Simplification

- Remove unnecessary gridlines.
- Remove redundant labels and repeated legends when the manuscript already explains them.
- Prefer direct labeling when it improves readability.
- Avoid chart junk, background decorations, and non-data ink.

## Figure Composition

- Keep margins balanced.
- Align panels cleanly in multi-panel figures.
- Use consistent spacing between panels.
- Keep captions outside the chart image unless the journal explicitly wants embedded labels only.
- Put figure captions below the figure, centered, using `Figure 1.Title text` style unless a journal overrides it.

## Journal-Like Baseline

When no stricter target is supplied, use this baseline:

- Times New Roman for English manuscript-facing text;
- `宋体` for Chinese body text and `黑体` for Chinese heading emphasis where needed;
- simple black or dark-gray axes;
- restrained line width;
- limited accent colors;
- readable panel lettering;
- export-ready layout with no ornamental styling.

## Audit Checklist

- fonts are consistent;
- sizes are consistent within the same role;
- colors are limited and interpretable;
- line weights are not arbitrary;
- panel labels are consistent;
- unnecessary decorative elements are removed.
